question: |
  Blocks A (3.0 kg) and B (5.0 kg) are connected by a light inextensible string on a rough horizontal surface, with B in
  front of A. A horizontal force of 40 N is applied to B. The coefficient of friction for each block is 0.20 and
  g = 9.81 m/s². Calculate (a) the total frictional force, (b) the acceleration, and (c) the tension in the string.

(a) $F = \mu (m_A + m_B) g = 0.20 \times 8.0 \times 9.81$ {M1}

$$ F = 15.7 \text{ N} $$ {A1}

(b) Whole system: $a = \frac{40 - 15.7}{8.0}$ {M1}

$$ a = 3.04 \text{ m s}^{-2} $$ {A1}

(c) Block $A$ alone: friction on $A = 0.20 \times 3.0 \times 9.81 = 5.89$ N, and $T - 5.89 = 3.0 \times 3.04$ {M1}

$$ \boxed{T = 15.0 \text{ N}} $$ {A1}
