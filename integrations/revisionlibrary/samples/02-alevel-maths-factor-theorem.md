question: |
  The polynomial f(x) = x³ + ax² + bx + 6, where a and b are constants, has (x - 2) as a factor.
  Given also that f(1) = 4, find the values of a and b, and hence find all three roots of f(x) = 0.
---
## Q5

$(x - 2)$ is a factor, so $f(2) = 0$:

$$ 8 + 4a + 2b + 6 = 0 \\ 4a + 2b = -14 \\ 2a + b = -7 $$ {M1 A1}

$f(1) = 4$:

$$ 1 + a + b + 6 = 4 \\ a + b = -3 $$ {M1}

Subtracting the equations: $a = -4$, then $b = -3 - (-4) = 1$ {A1}

So $f(x) = x^3 - 4x^2 + x + 6$. Compare coefficients with $(x-2)(x^2 + px - 3)$:

$$ x^2: \quad p - 2 = -4 \quad \Rightarrow \quad p = -2 \\ f(x) = (x - 2)(x^2 - 2x - 3) $$ {M1}

$$ f(x) = (x - 2)(x - 3)(x + 1) $$

$$ \boxed{x = -1, \quad x = 2, \quad x = 3} $$ {A1}

> Check: $f(3) = 27 - 36 + 3 + 6 = 0$ ✓
