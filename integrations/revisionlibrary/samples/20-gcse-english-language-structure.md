## Q3

The writer opens with a time reference, "had been running for a month before anyone believed it was real", which suggests disbelief and denial about the closure. {B1}

The middle shows the regulars carrying on out of habit, commenting on "the same faded signs", which builds a sense of routine before reality arrives. {B1}

The shift comes with "until the day the shelves themselves began coming down", where the physical change finally forces the truth on everyone. {B1}

The focus then narrows from the crowd to one person, Mrs Ahmed behind the counter. {B1}

This personalises the closure and makes the reader feel its human cost. {B1}

The extract ends on her slow, final task, "taking longer over it than the task could possibly require", suggesting she is reluctant to let the moment end. {B1}
