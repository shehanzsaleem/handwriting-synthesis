question: |
  A buffer solution is made from 500 cm³ of a solution containing 0.200 mol ethanoic acid and 0.150 mol sodium ethanoate
  (Ka of ethanoic acid = 1.74 × 10⁻⁵ mol dm⁻³). Calculate the pH of this buffer. Then calculate the new pH after
  10.0 cm³ of 1.00 mol dm⁻³ hydrochloric acid is added to it.
---
## Q8

$$ [\text{H}^+] = K_a \times \frac{n(\text{acid})}{n(\text{salt})} = 1.74 \times 10^{-5} \times \frac{0.200}{0.150} $$ {M1}

$$ = 2.32 \times 10^{-5} \quad \Rightarrow \quad \text{pH} = -\log(2.32 \times 10^{-5}) = \boxed{4.63} $$ {A1}

$$ n(\text{HCl}) = \frac{10.0}{1000} \times 1.00 = 0.0100 \text{ mol} $$

The added acid reacts with the ethanoate ions:

$$ \ce{CH3COO- + H+ -> CH3COOH} $$ {M1}

$$ n(\text{acid}) = 0.200 + 0.0100 = 0.210 \quad n(\text{salt}) = 0.150 - 0.0100 = 0.140 $$ {M1}

$$ [\text{H}^+] = 1.74 \times 10^{-5} \times \frac{0.210}{0.140} = 2.61 \times 10^{-5} $$ {M1}

$$ \text{pH} = \boxed{4.58} $$ {A1}

> Both are in the same total volume, so the mole ratio can be used directly.
