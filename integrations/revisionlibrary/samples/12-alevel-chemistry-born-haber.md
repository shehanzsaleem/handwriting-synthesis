question: |
  Use a Born-Haber cycle and the data below to calculate the lattice enthalpy of formation of calcium oxide, CaO.
  Enthalpy of formation of CaO(s) = -635 kJ/mol; atomisation of Ca(s) = +178; 1st ionisation energy of Ca = +590;
  2nd ionisation energy of Ca = +1145; atomisation of O2(g), per mole of O atoms = +249; 1st electron affinity of
  O = -141; 2nd electron affinity of O = +798 (all kJ/mol).

Hess's law: $\Delta H_f = (\text{sum of the other steps}) + \Delta H_{latt}$ {M1}

$$ \text{Ca}: \; 178 + 590 + 1145 = +1913 \text{ kJ/mol} $$ {M1}

$$ \text{O}: \; 249 + (-141) + 798 = +906 \text{ kJ/mol} $$ {M1}

$$ 1913 + 906 = +2819 \text{ kJ/mol} $$ {A1}

$$ \Delta H_{latt} = -635 - 2819 $$ {M1}

$$ \boxed{\Delta H_{latt} = -3454 \text{ kJ/mol}} $$ {A1}
